Partners

Two partnerships for your Odoo

Ringover: cloud telephony connected to the CRM

As an official partner of Ringover, a leader in business cloud telephony, we improve customer experience and team efficiency.

  • CTI: computer-telephony integration with your CRM
  • IVR: better greeting and routing of inbound calls
  • Advanced call analytics
  • Built-in collaboration tools for unified communication

Leexi.ai: meeting AI, synced with Odoo

Leexi does more than transcribe: it structures what is said in your meetings and feeds it into your CRM.

  • AI-generated summaries, action items and key points
  • Native Odoo integration: insights flow into the CRM
  • Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom or in person
  • GDPR-compliant and ISO 27001-certified
