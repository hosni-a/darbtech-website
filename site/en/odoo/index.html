FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does “Odoo Gold Partner” mean?

Gold is a level in Odoo’s partner programme. DarbTech reached it on 22 September 2026, after more than ten years as an Odoo partner (since 2015).

Which Odoo versions do you work with?

From V8 to the latest releases, on both the Community and Enterprise editions.

Are your consultants certified?

Yes. All our consultants and developers are Odoo-certified. Some also hold ISTQB, SAFe, CSPO, Lean Six Sigma or Ringover certifications.

Where are your teams based?

In Paris (DarbTech Labs) and Rabat (DarbTech). We work with French- and English-speaking companies.

Let’s talk about your Odoo project

A certified consultant will call you back to understand your needs, with no commitment. Our teams work from Paris and Rabat.
