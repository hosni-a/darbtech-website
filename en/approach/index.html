An Agile, transparent and industrialised approach

Five steps to roll out Odoo and four principles to run them: this is how we have supported our clients since 2015, from scoping to maintenance.

The 5 steps

We support you throughout the roll-out

From studying your business to maintaining your new tool, each step produces concrete deliverables that you sign off.

  1. 01

    Study and analysis

    We study your business and procedures, department by department, including how departments work together and with your existing business software.

    • Current-state review
    • Process map
    • Integrations to plan
  2. 02

    Process alignment

    We align your procedures with the ERP’s logic to get the most from standard Odoo, without losing what makes you different.

    • Target processes
    • Standard vs custom decisions
  3. 03

    Implementation

    We configure the standard apps and build the custom modules that fit the tool to the way you work.

    • Configuration
    • Custom modules
    • Testing

    Training and support

    We train and support every team, from first steps to everyday use.

    • Role-based training
    • Go-live support

Our principles
