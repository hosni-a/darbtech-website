Engagement in the digitization of the business process.
[H1] Engagement in the digitization of the business process.
ERP ODO
 January 28, 2024
 by
 DarbTech Team
---section container pt-4 pb-5 ---
PUTHOD, a company based in the Pays de Savoie region specializing in the landscaping services sector, has approached us to digitize its business processes and eliminate the manual completion and input of forms into the computer system. 
Issue : 
- Multiple mobile teams complete paper forms on a daily basis.
- The back-office consolidates all the paper forms to generate site reports.
- There is a significant risk of missing crucial information.
Solution : 
- Implementation of standard Odoo modules.
- Development of specific modules to align with business processes.
- Consolidation of all relevant information from a landscaping project into a single tool accessible to all personnel, resulting in increased productivity and efficiency!
