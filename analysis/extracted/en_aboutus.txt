---section s_text_image o_colored_level pt32 pb0---
[H2] DarbTech
DarbTech is a company based in Paris and Rabat. We work with small and medium-sized businesses (SMB) in French and English-speaking regions worldwide. We assist you in various areas, such as:
-Improving the productivity of your business through digitization solutions with CRM and ERP.
-Providing dedicated teams for specific application development.
DarbTech was born from the founders' desire to provide value-added services to stakeholders while facilitating exchanges in this vast village our world has become.

 Our Methodology
---section s_text_cover o_colored_level pb48---
[H3] DevOps
Our DevOps methodology ensures the industrialization of our processes. It ensures operational excellence, promotes innovation, and guarantees efficiency at the core of each project while adhering to continuous improvement.
[H3] Agile-Scrum
Our Agile methodology is one of the pillars of our success. It ensures operational excellence, promotes innovation, and guarantees efficiency at the core of each project. Short digital development cycles enable alignment with the involved client's needs, resulting in satisfaction and tangible benefits for their business.
---section s_text_cover o_colored_level pb128---
[H3] Transparency
We provide our employees and clients with project tracking and development tools that enable clear visibility and readability of the actions taken and the associated costs. At any time, we can allocate resources exclusively to a project or work collaboratively through our partnerships and networks of experts.
[H3] Commitment
We acknowledge that every project may encounter challenges and uncertainties. We strive to de-risk every project and have a strong commitment to ensuring the success of our services. Like a bridge of honor, we collaborate for our clients' satisfaction, regardless of the path taken. We are your companion on the journey.

[H3] 
[H3] Odoo
 DarbTech, Certified Odoo Partner Since 2015!
For nearly a decade, DarbTech has been proud to be a Certified Odoo Partner, supporting clients across various versions, from V8 to the latest Enterprise and Community Editions. 
 We have been both witnesses and active contributors to the significant evolution of this essential ERP, designed to meet the growing needs of SMBs, SMEs, and large enterprise groups.

Our added value?
Proven expertise gained from years of supporting diverse projects.
The ability to advise and implement tailored solutions, regardless of the industry.
A focus on efficiency, innovation, and customer satisfaction.
 At DarbTech, every project is a new opportunity to showcase our expertise and contribute to the sustainable growth of our partners.
[H3] Ringover
DarbTech, Official Ringover Partner for Outstanding Communication!
At DarbTech, we are proud of our strategic partnership with Ringover, a leader in cloud telephony solutions for businesses. Together, we strive to transform and optimize customer experience as well as operational efficiency for companies.
Our solutions include:
CTI (Computer Telephony Integration): Seamless integration with CRM systems for optimal customer interaction management.
IVR (Interactive Voice Response): Improved call reception and direction for incoming inquiries.
Advanced Analytics: Monitoring and analyzing telephony performance for better decision-making.
Integrated Collaborative Tools: Unified communication to enhance team productivity.
Through this collaboration, DarbTech delivers innovative solutions tailored to the specific needs of each organization, ensuring an optimal user experience and enhanced performance.
[H3] Leexi.ai
DarbTech and Leexi: A Partnership to Boost Your Productivity and Collaboration
By integrating conversational AI into the heart of your work environment, you free up valuable time while ensuring the quality of your follow-ups. Leexi doesn't just transcribe; it structures the intelligence of your meetings.
Increased Productivity: Say goodbye to manual note-taking. AI generates concise summaries, identifies action items, and instantly extracts key points.
Seamless Collaboration: Thanks to native integration with Odoo, critical information flows directly into your CRM, ensuring the entire team is aligned without any administrative effort.
 Full Compatibility: Whether your meetings take place on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or even in person, Leexi accurately captures every exchange.
Security and Compliance: Work with complete peace of mind. The solution is fully GDPR compliant and ISO 27001 certified, guaranteeing maximum protection of your sensitive data.
